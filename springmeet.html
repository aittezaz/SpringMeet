Questions

Frequently asked

What if only one person accepts?+
If only one person taps Accept, they see "Waiting for the other person…" — and wait until either the other accepts or the timer runs out. If time expires without mutual acceptance, the conversation disappears completely from both sides.
Are expired chats really deleted?+
Yes. When a session expires without mutual acceptance, all messages are permanently deleted and hidden from both users. No archive, no history. This is by design — it removes pressure and protects privacy.
Is SpringMeet safe for 18+ adults?+
SpringMeet is strictly 18+ only. We require age confirmation and Terms of Service acceptance during sign-up, use AI moderation on every message, have a human moderation team, a three-strike warning system, and instant block/report features.
Can I control who I match with?+
By default, matching is global and random — that's where the magic happens. You can optionally filter by language, region, interests, and conversation vibe. Blocked users are permanently excluded from your queue.
How do calls work after acceptance?+
After mutual acceptance, you can initiate voice or video calls from your inbox at any time. The other person must accept the call request. All calls use encrypted WebRTC — private and peer-to-peer where possible.
Is SpringMeet free?+
The core experience is completely free — matching, chatting, inbox, and calls. Premium features like boosted matching priority, extra filters, and profile themes are planned for the future, but the heart of SpringMeet will always be free.
What happens to my data?+
We never sell your data to anyone. Expired chats are deleted. Accepted chats are stored securely only for the people involved. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings.

About SpringMeet

About SpringMeet

Our Story

SpringMeet was born from a simple belief: that the most meaningful connections happen when we're forced to be present and real. In a world of endless scrolling and ghosting, we wanted to create something different — a place where every conversation matters because it has a beginning, a middle, and either a permanent bond or a clean goodbye.

The 15-minute timer isn't a gimmick. It's a design principle. Scarcity creates value. When you know this conversation might vanish, you stop overthinking and start being yourself.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Data we collect

We collect your email, username, country, age, and optional profile details. We collect conversation metadata (timestamps, session outcomes) but NOT expired chat content — that is permanently deleted. We collect abuse/moderation events to keep the platform safe.

How we use your data

To match you with other users, to protect the community through moderation, to send you important account notifications, and to improve the product. We never sell your data to advertisers or third parties.

Expired conversations

When a timed session expires without mutual acceptance, all message content is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our servers. Only metadata (session existed, outcome: expired) is retained for safety analytics.

Your rights

You can request your data, correct it, or delete your account at any time from Settings. Account deletion removes all personal data within 30 days. Contact us: aittezazahmad@gmail.com
